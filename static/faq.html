What Is Kertoons.com?

Kertoons.com is a cutting-edge AI-powered storybook creation platform designed specifically for schools, educators, and students. It empowers learners to craft imaginative stories using simple prompts, transforming them into cartoon-style illustrated PDF books - all under the school's own branding.

Key Features That Make Kertoons the Best

Student Story Creation

  • Students enter a story prompt, and Kertoons' AI generates vivid cartoon scenes.
  • Professionally formatted PDF storybooks, ready to download or share.

Multilingual Support

  • Supports English, Malayalam, Hindi, Spanish, and more languages.
  • Promotes language acquisition and cultural storytelling.

White-Label Branding for Schools

  • Custom logos, names, and color schemes for every school.
  • Perfect for districts, private institutions, and nonprofits.

Mobile-Friendly Dashboard

  • Built with jQuery Mobile for seamless use across devices.
  • Create stories anytime, anywhere.

Stories with more number of scenes

  • You can create stories with more scenes.
  • Your school administrator has the ability to set the scene count. Please note that if your story includes more than 5 scenes, additional credits will be used, with one credit charged per scene.

Privacy-First, Multi-User Architecture

  • Secure, private workspace for each school.
  • Teachers and creators only see their own content.

Important Notice for Educators and Parents

While we take extensive precautions to ensure that AI-generated images are appropriate for children, it is important to recognize that no system is infallible. Despite our safeguards, there remains a small possibility that certain images may contain elements that are unsuitable for young audiences.

We strongly advise that all AI-generated visuals be reviewed by a parent, guardian, or responsible adult before being shared with children or published as part of any story. This includes classroom use, digital storytelling, and public-facing content.

Although we have not encountered inappropriate content across thousands of images created for our stories, we believe in proactive responsibility. Adult supervision and final review are essential steps to ensure that every image aligns with the values and sensitivities of your audience.

Thank you for helping us maintain a safe and enriching creative environment for all.
