Help / Q&A

Do AI cartoon generators have issues with image consistency across story pages?

Yes, many AI cartoon platforms struggle with visual consistency - characters and settings may look different from page to page. Kertoons solves this by using a proprietary image consistency engine. All images in a story are generated with shared visual parameters, ensuring that characters, backgrounds, and styles remain consistent throughout the entire book.

How can teachers create cartoon storybooks without design skills?

Kertoons lets educators enter a simple story prompt. Our AI generates cartoon-style scenes and assembles a beautiful PDF storybook - no design experience needed.

Can AI cartoon tools support multiple languages for diverse classrooms?

Yes. Kertoons supports English, Malayalam, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic and more languages - making it ideal for multilingual learning environments.

How do I ensure student data is safe when using AI tools?

Kertoons offers a privacy-first platform. Each school has its own secure space, and teachers only see their own content. No cross-school data sharing.

Can I add my school's logo and colors to AI-generated content?

Absolutely. Kertoons is fully white-labeled - your school's branding appears on every page of the storybook.

Is there a mobile-friendly AI cartoon platform for students?

Kertoons features a jQuery Mobile dashboard, allowing students and teachers to create stories from any device.

What if I need to review student-generated stories before publishing?

Kertoons includes a review and publish option so educators can approve each story's content before sharing.

Important Notice for Educators and Parents

While we take extensive precautions to ensure that AI-generated images are appropriate for children, it is important to recognize that no system is infallible. Despite our safeguards, there remains a small possibility that certain images may contain elements that are unsuitable for young audiences.

We strongly advise that all AI-generated visuals be reviewed by a parent, guardian, or responsible adult before being shared with children or published as part of any story. This includes classroom use, digital storytelling, and public-facing content.

Although we have not encountered inappropriate content across thousands of images created for our stories, we believe in proactive responsibility. Adult supervision and final review are essential steps to ensure that every image aligns with the values and sensitivities of your audience.

Thank you for helping us maintain a safe and enriching creative environment for all.
